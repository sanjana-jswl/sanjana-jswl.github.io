My Project

Nutri Tracker

Nutri Tracker is a diet and nutrition tracking website developed as a college project. It helps users monitor their daily food intake and keep track of their nutrition. The website aims to encourage healthy eating habits and make nutrition tracking simple and convenient. Unlike many other nutrition tracking platforms, Nutri Tracker is designed to provide its features without requiring users to purchase a subscription. It focuses on making daily nutrition tracking accessible and affordable for everyone. The main goal is to help users become more aware of their eating habits and make healthier food choices.
